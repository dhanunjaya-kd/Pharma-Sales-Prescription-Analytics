## Day 2 - SQL Analytics

### Tasks Completed

* Created SQLite database
* Loaded all CSV files into database tables
* Verified table creation and data loading
* Performed business analysis using SQL

### Business Queries Completed

1. Top Products by Revenue
2. Top Doctors by Prescriptions
3. Top Medical Representatives by Revenue
4. Monthly Revenue Trend
5. Territory Performance Analysis
6. Prescription vs Sales Analysis

### Key Insights

* Chennai generated the highest overall revenue.
* Delhi was the second-best performing territory.
* Several products generated over 1 million in revenue.
* Monthly revenue remained relatively stable across the period.
* Neurologists and Gynecologists generated the highest prescription volumes.
